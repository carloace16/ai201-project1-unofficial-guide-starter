The Boruto anime has a massive amount of what the studio calls "anime canon" — basically episodes that aren't in the manga but are supposedly officially recognized. If you want to catch up quickly and only watch the pure manga storyline, you only need to watch about 80 episodes out of the 293. Watch the Momoshiki Arc (Ep 51-66), the Mujina Bandits (148-151), the Vessel/Kara arcs (181-220), and the Code arc (287-293).
