One Punch Man is confusing to catch up on because there are technically three versions. There is ONE's original webcomic (rough art, furthest along in the story), Yusuke Murata's manga adaptation (god-tier art), and the anime. If you just finished Season 2 of the anime, start reading the Murata manga at Chapter 85. Ignore the webcomic unless you really want spoilers for what happens five arcs from now.
