The Hashira Training Arc in Demon Slayer is a weird one. In the manga, it is incredibly short—only 9 chapters. The anime studio decided to stretch this into an entire 8-episode season by adding a ton of anime-original training sequences. If you just want the plot, read chapters 128 through 136. Otherwise, the anime is mostly just a fun slice-of-life breather before the final Infinity Castle movie trilogy.
