[Anime Catch-Up Discord - #One-Piece]
For anyone starting the Dressrosa arc (Episode 629), I highly recommend switching to "One Pace." It is a fan project that edits the official anime to match the manga's pacing. The official anime stretches 102 manga chapters into 118 episodes by adding three-minute reaction shots. One Pace cuts the watch time in half without losing any actual canon story.
