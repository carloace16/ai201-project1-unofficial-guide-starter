Before starting the Bleach: Thousand-Year Blood War (TYBW) anime, you do NOT need to watch the Bount Arc or any of the other filler arcs from the original 2004 anime. However, you should probably watch a 10-minute YouTube recap of the Fullbringer Arc. The TYBW anime assumes you perfectly remember who all the characters are from 10 years ago and drops you right into the action with zero recap.
